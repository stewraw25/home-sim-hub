Radar vs camera golf launch monitors compared (UK)

Last updated 24 Nov 2026. Researched from makers' published documents and Amazon UK listings. We have not tested these products.

Quick answer

Should you pick a radar or camera launch monitor for a UK room?

Pick a beside-the-ball camera unit if your room is under 4.3 m deep, because it sits ~0.4 to 0.5 m beside the tee. Pick a behind-the-ball radar unit if you have 4.3 to 4.9 m+ of indoor depth or share the bay between left- and right-handed golfers without moving the monitor.

Choosing a launch monitor brand starts with where the hardware sits. Behind-the-ball radar and beside-the-ball camera units place different demands on room depth, lighting and dual-handed setups.

Frequently asked questions

Which launch monitor type works for both left- and right-handed players without moving the unit?

Behind-the-ball radar units such as the Garmin Approach R10, Rapsodo MLM2PRO and FlightScope Mevo+ sit centred behind the golfer, so left- and right-handed players do not need to move the hardware between shots.

Why do camera launch monitors need less room depth?

Photometric camera units sit 0.4 to 0.5 m beside the hitting zone instead of 1.8 to 2.7 m behind the golfer, saving roughly 2 m of floor depth.
