Updated 2026-11-24. Researched from makers' published documents and Amazon UK listings. We have not tested these products.

Before you commit

  • Check whether the software needs a Windows gaming PC (for example, Uneekor EYE MINI LITE requires a Windows PC and Ethernet cable) or runs on a tablet or built-in screen.
  • Check regional terms: Bushnell notes that US-spec Launch Pro units require a US internet connection every 45 days, so UK buyers should check UK/EU sourcing.
  • Verify current annual subscription pricing directly on the maker's UK page before ordering hardware.

Frequently asked questions

Which launch monitors work out of the box with no ongoing fee?

Shot Scope LM1 includes the Shot Scope mobile app free for life. FlightScope Mevo+ charges no FlightScope subscription and bundles E6 courses. Foresight GC3 (£7,500 UK retail) includes FSX Play with no recurring subscription.

Does GSPro require a brand subscription as well?

It depends on the brand. FlightScope Mevo+ supports direct GSPro connection without a FlightScope fee, whereas Uneekor EYE MINI / LITE and Bushnell Launch Pro require an active paid software tier from the hardware maker in addition to a GSPro licence.
