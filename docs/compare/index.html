Launch monitor brand profiles

Garmin (R10 & R50)

Radar & Camera

Covers both entry-level portable radar and standalone touchscreen camera simulation.

  • Approach R10 (£377.39): IPX7 radar, 10h battery, 1.8–2.4 m behind tee. Amazon listing notes international version sold from abroad.
  • Approach R50 (£4,299.00): 3-camera unit beside tee (~0.5 m) with built-in 10" colour touchscreen and 4h battery.
  • Software note: Virtual rounds require a Garmin Golf subscription.
R10 on Amazon → R50 vs ST MAX

Rapsodo (MLM2PRO)

Dual Camera + Radar

Combines Doppler radar behind the golfer with dual optical cameras for shot & impact video.

  • MLM2PRO (£399.00): Sits 6.5–8.5 ft behind the ball, with at least 8 ft to net (over 14 ft total depth).
  • Key feature: Shot Vision and Impact Vision swing replay in the Rapsodo app.
  • Ceiling guidance: Rapsodo notes 9 ft may fit players around 5 ft 8 in and under; taller players may need 10–11 ft.
MLM2PRO on Amazon → Under £1,000 guide

SkyTrak (ST MAX)

Beside-Ball Photometric

Long-standing indoor simulator brand placing sensors beside the tee to save room depth.

  • ST MAX (£1,699.00): Dual Doppler radar + photometric cameras; Amazon UK listing includes 1-year software and GOLFTEC speed training.
  • Space requirement: SkyTrak lists a 10 ft x 10 ft minimum unobstructed area and 16–17 ft depth for full enclosures.
  • Left/right handers: Unit moves to the opposite side of the mat for left-handed golfers.
ST MAX on Amazon → Compare with R50

Square Golf (Indoor & Omni)

Compact Camera

Dedicated indoor camera units priced between entry radar monitors and higher-end commercial units.

  • Square Golf indoor (£649.00): Compact indoor unit under £700 when checked.
  • Square Golf Omni (£1,599.99): Beside-the-golfer placement (~14–20 in from tee; retailer Shop Indoor Golf suggests 9 ft H x 10 ft W x 12 ft D).
Square Indoor on Amazon → Omni on Amazon

Shot Scope (LM1)

Entry Doppler Radar

UK golf-tech brand offering a compact screen-equipped radar monitor with no app subscription.

  • LM1 (£179.00): K-band 24 GHz radar placed ~1.4–1.5 m (55 in) behind the ball; 300 g, ~5h USB-C battery, IPX3 rated.
  • Display & data: 3.5" colour screen showing ball speed, club speed, smash factor, carry and total distance.
  • Software note: Free lifetime Shot Scope mobile app; built for range and net practice rather than full course simulation.
LM1 on Amazon → Under £1,000 guide

Uneekor (EYE MINI & LITE)

Dual IR Cameras

Known for high-speed impact video (Club Optix) and dimple-reading ball tracking without marked balls.

  • EYE MINI LITE (£1,699–£1,999 Uneekor UK): Indoor-only wired unit (mains power + CAT-6 Ethernet to Windows PC); 19 data points; 12" x 8" hitting zone.
  • EYE MINI (£2,999 Uneekor UK): Portable version with 6–8h battery, on-unit display and iOS VIEW Air support.
  • Software note: Includes Player Package; GSPro / 3rd-party simulation and AI Trainer require a paid tier.
Uneekor UK specs →

FlightScope (Mevo Gen2 & Mevo+)

3D Doppler Radar

Radar systems placed behind the tee, suited to dual-handed bays because nothing sits beside the mat.

  • Mevo+ (£1,759 UK retail) & Gen2 (~£1,199): Placed 7–9 ft behind the tee with 8–13 ft indoor ball flight recommended.
  • Ball requirement: Uses metallic dot stickers or Titleist RCT balls indoors for spin measurement.
  • Software note: No mandatory FlightScope subscription; Mevo+ bundles E6 courses and integrates directly with GSPro.

Bushnell & Foresight (Launch Pro / GC3)

Triscopic 3-Camera

Sibling hardware platforms built on Foresight's three-camera infrared tracking beside the ball.

  • Bushnell Launch Pro (£2,490 Bushnell Europe): Lower upfront hardware cost; club data and simulation require an annual Foresight+ membership. Check UK/EU vs US unit terms carefully (Bushnell notes US Launch Pro units require a US internet check every 45 days).
  • Foresight GC3 (£7,500 UK retail): Unlocked outright purchase with FSX Play included and no recurring subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Which launch monitor brands need the least room depth?

Photometric camera units that sit beside the ball — including Garmin Approach R50, SkyTrak ST MAX, Square Golf, Bushnell Launch Pro, Foresight GC3 and Uneekor EYE MINI / EYE MINI LITE — do not need 1.8 to 2.7 m of open floor behind the golfer. You still need safe ball-to-screen distance and full swing clearance.

Which launch monitor brands have no mandatory subscription?

Shot Scope LM1 uses the free Shot Scope mobile app with no subscription. Foresight GC3 (sold in the UK at £7,500) includes FSX Play software with no recurring fee. FlightScope Mevo+ includes bundled E6 courses and supports direct GSPro connection without a FlightScope subscription, though third-party sim software licences still apply separately.

Are Amazon product prices on this page live?

No. Amazon UK prices shown here were checked on 24 Nov 2026 and change frequently. Always check Amazon UK or the maker's UK store for the current price before buying.

Do you test these simulator brands hands-on?

No. We research every figure from makers' manuals, official UK product pages and Amazon UK listings, and we have not tested these products ourselves.
