Quick answer

Which sim racing brand fits PS5 vs PC and small UK rooms?

Logitech G923 (£199.99), Thrustmaster T300 RS GT (£299.99), Logitech G RS50 (£499.99) and Fanatec GT DD Pro (£504.95) list PlayStation and PC support, while Fanatec CSL DD (£264.99) is PC-only. For small rooms, Playseat Challenge ActiFit folds to 136 x 54 x 96 cm (9 kg).

Sim racing hardware splits into wheel bases & pedals (Logitech G, Thrustmaster, Fanatec, MOZA) and mounting frames or cockpits (Grefinity, Playseat, Next Level Racing, GT Omega). Every price and platform note below comes from Amazon UK listings checked on 24 Nov 2026 and the makers' support pages.

Before you mix brands

  • Confirm console compatibility before buying: PlayStation licensing sits inside the wheel base, while PC setups can mix USB pedal sets across brands.
  • If choosing a direct drive wheel base, check the cockpit maker's mounting template and torque guidance first.

Frequently asked questions

Which sim racing wheel brands work on PS5 as well as PC?

Among our checked Amazon UK listings, the Logitech G923 (PS5/PC), Thrustmaster T300 RS GT (PS5/PS4/PC), Logitech G RS50 (PS5/PC) and Fanatec Gran Turismo DD Pro (PC/PS5/PS4) list PlayStation compatibility. The Fanatec CSL DD listing is PC.

Which cockpit brands offer foldable storage for smaller UK rooms?

Playseat offers the Challenge ActiFit (136 x 54 x 96 cm, 9 kg) and Next Level Racing offers the GTLite Pro, both built to fold away between sessions.
