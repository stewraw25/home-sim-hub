Safety & clearance checks

  • Leave the maker's stated free space behind any net or impact screen so balls cannot rebound off or strike a wall behind the fabric.
  • Garmin's launch monitor manuals note that enclosure frames should be padded to reduce ricochet, and that radar units placed behind a mat should sit level with or above the hitting surface.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a practice net and a simulator enclosure?

A practice net such as FORZA or Amazon Basics catches the ball for driving-range style sessions. A simulator enclosure such as the SimSpace golf simulator enclosure includes side netting, frame padding and an impact screen built to take a projected image.

Which hitting mat size gives room to stand on the same surface?

The GIRAFFYCO mat listed on Amazon UK measures 1.5 x 1.2 m so you can stand and hit on one mat, whereas compact strips like the Spurk Winter Strike Mat only sit under the ball.
