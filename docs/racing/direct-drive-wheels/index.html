Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between geared and direct drive sim racing wheels?

In a direct drive base such as the Fanatec CSL DD, MOZA R5 or Logitech G RS50, the wheel rim mounts directly to the motor shaft. Entry kits like the Logitech G923 drive the rim through internal gears.

Do direct drive wheel bases work on PS5 as well as PC?

Only console-licensed models such as the Fanatec Gran Turismo DD Pro and Logitech G RS50 list PS5/PS4 compatibility; PC-only bases like the Fanatec CSL DD (PC) do not.
