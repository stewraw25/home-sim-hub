The parts of a sim racing set-up

  1. Wheel base and wheel, either geared or belt-driven, or direct drive.
  2. Pedals, often bundled with the wheel; some upgrades use a load cell brake.
  3. Mount: a desk clamp, a wheel stand, or a full cockpit with a seat.
  4. Screen: a TV, one monitor or three, often on a monitor stand.
  5. Extras such as a shifter or handbrake.
