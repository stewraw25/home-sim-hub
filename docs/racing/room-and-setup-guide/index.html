Three ways to mount a wheel

Desk clamp

Many geared wheels clamp to a desk. It takes no extra floor space, but the desk needs to stay put under force, and your chair needs to stay still when you brake.

Wheel stand

A folding stand holds the wheel and pedals and can be stored away. You sit on your own chair or sofa.

Before you buy

  1. Measure the floor area and leave space to get in and out.
  2. Check the cockpit's supported wheel bases and pedals on the maker's page.
  3. Check the cockpit's weight limit and recommended driver height.
  4. Check console or PC compatibility for the wheel and pedals.

Frequently asked questions

How much floor space does a foldable racing cockpit take?

Playseat lists the Challenge ActiFit at 136 x 54 x 96 cm (L x W x H) when unfolded, weighing 9 kg, and folds away between sessions.

Can a folding stand handle a direct drive wheel?

Some folding frames flex under high torque. Next Level Racing states its F-GT includes wheel and pedal strengthening kits for direct drive wheels; always verify wheel base compatibility on the cockpit maker's site.
