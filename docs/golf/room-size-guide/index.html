How much room do you need for a golf simulator? (UK guide)

Last updated 24 Nov 2026. Researched from makers' published documents and Amazon UK listings. We have not tested these products.

Quick answer

How much space does a UK home golf simulator need?

Makers cite 2.74 m to 3.05 m+ (9 to 10 ft+) ceiling height measured from the top of the hitting mat, 3.05 to 3.7 m (10 to 12 ft) width, and either ~3.7 m+ depth for beside-the-ball camera units or 4.3 to 4.9 m+ (14 to 16 ft+) depth for behind-the-ball radar units.

Three measurements decide whether a golf simulator fits: ceiling height, room width and room depth. Each launch monitor maker publishes its own figures, so start with the one you're considering.

The quickest check, which SkyTrak suggests: stand where you'd hit, take a slow practice swing with your driver, and see whether you clear the ceiling, walls and lights.

Feet are the makers' units. Metric conversions are ours: 8 ft is about 2.4 m, 9 ft about 2.7 m, 10 ft about 3.05 m and 12 ft about 3.7 m.

Ceiling height

None of these makers gives one ceiling height that suits everyone, because it depends on your height, your swing and the club. SkyTrak says a 10 ft ceiling typically offers ample clearance and that many golfers say 9 ft is enough. Rapsodo says a 9 ft ceiling may suit golfers about 5 ft 8 in and under, taller golfers may need 10 or 11 ft, and suggests allowing 6 to 9 in above the highest point of your swing.

Measure from the top of your hitting mat, not the floor, and to the lowest thing overhead, such as a beam, light or garage door track.

Width

SkyTrak says its screens and nets range from 8 to 13 ft wide and suggests a 12 ft room width for a more spacious feel, plus an extra 1 to 2 ft if both left- and right-handed golfers will use it. Camera units that sit beside the ball need moving to the other side for a left-handed golfer.

Depth

Depth is where radar and camera units differ most.

  • Radar behind the golfer: add the space behind the ball for the unit to the ball-to-net distance. For the Garmin R10, that's 6 to 8 ft plus at least 8 ft. For the Rapsodo MLM2PRO, Rapsodo puts the indoor total at a little more than 14 ft.
  • Camera beside the ball: the unit takes no extra depth behind you, but you still need room for your stance and backswing, the ball-to-screen distance, and the screen maker's clearance behind the screen. SkyTrak suggests 17 ft of room depth for a comfortable set-up, and gives a minimum of 16 ft for its Studio 10 enclosure.

Before you buy

  1. Measure ceiling height, width and depth in metres and feet.
  2. Check the launch monitor maker's set-up page for the exact model.
  3. Check the net, screen or enclosure listing for its own size and the space it needs behind it.
  4. Do the slow driver swing test in the space.

Frequently asked questions

What ceiling height do you need for an indoor golf simulator?

SkyTrak says a 10 ft (3.05 m) ceiling typically offers ample clearance and many golfers manage with 9 ft (2.74 m). Rapsodo says 9 ft may suit golfers around 5 ft 8 in and under, while taller players may need 10 or 11 ft. Measure from the top of your hitting mat to the lowest overhead obstruction.

How much room depth does a radar unit need vs a camera unit?

Radar monitors like the Garmin R10 and Rapsodo MLM2PRO sit 6 to 8.5 ft behind the ball plus at least 8 ft of ball flight (14 ft+ total). Camera units sit beside the ball, saving behind-the-golfer depth, though SkyTrak still recommends 16 to 17 ft overall depth for full simulator enclosures.

What extra width is needed for both left- and right-handed golfers?

SkyTrak suggests adding 1 to 2 ft of extra width when both left- and right-handed golfers use the bay. Beside-the-ball camera units also need moving to the opposite side of the hitting area.
