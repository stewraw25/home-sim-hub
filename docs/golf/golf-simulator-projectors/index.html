Golf simulator projectors: throw ratios and brightness (UK)

Last updated 24 Nov 2026. Researched from makers' published documents and Amazon UK listings. We have not tested these products.

Quick answer

Which short-throw projector throw ratios fit a UK bay?

For a 2.4 m wide 16:9 impact screen, the BenQ TH671ST (£749.00, 3,000 lm) and laser BenQ AH700ST (£1,749.00, 4,000 lm) mount roughly 1.66 to 1.99 m away (0.69–0.83 throw ratio), while the Optoma GT1080HDR (£795.99, 3,800 lm) mounts around 1.18 m away (0.49:1 fixed).

A short-throw projector lets you mount the unit ahead of or just above the hitting area so the golfer does not cast a shadow onto the impact screen. Three 1080p models were listed on Amazon UK when we checked on 24 Nov 2026.

Choosing between zoom and fixed lenses

  • 1.2x optical zoom (BenQ TH671ST / AH700ST): Gives placement flexibility across a 1.66 to 1.99 m range for a 2.4 m wide screen, helpful if ceiling joists sit in a fixed position.
  • 0.49:1 fixed short-throw (Optoma GT1080HDR): Sits closer to the screen (~1.18 m for 2.4 m width), keeping it further ahead of a tall golfer's driver swing, with exact positioning set by mount distance.
  • Keystone & screen fill: BenQ's AH700ST adds 2D horizontal/vertical keystone (±30°) and Screen Fill presets (16:9, 16:10, 4:3, 1:1) for non-widescreen enclosures.

Frequently asked questions

How far from the impact screen does a short-throw projector sit?

Multiply your screen width by the projector's throw ratio. For a 2.4 m wide image, a 0.69 to 0.83 lens (BenQ TH671ST and AH700ST) sits roughly 1.66 m to 1.99 m from the screen, whereas a 0.49 lens (Optoma GT1080HDR) sits around 1.18 m away.

Is 3,000 to 4,000 lumens bright enough for a garage golf simulator?

BenQ notes 3,000 ANSI lumens on the TH671ST keeps the image visible with room lights on, while the laser AH700ST increases output to 4,000 ANSI lumens.
