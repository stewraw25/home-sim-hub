Quick answer

What do you need for simple net-and-mat home golf practice?

Pair a 2.1 m to 3 m practice net or steel cage (such as FORZA, Amazon Basics, SimSpace or Hillman PGM) with a protective hitting mat and leave the net maker's recommended clearance behind the fabric.

A net and a mat are the simplest home practice set-up, and they're also part of most indoor simulator bays.

Frequently asked questions

Where should a radar launch monitor sit relative to a hitting mat?

Garmin's Approach R10 manual notes that the bottom edge of the launch monitor should sit above the top surface of the hitting mat.

Do I need a hitting mat if I use a golf net in the garden?

Yes. A hitting mat protects your lawn from divots and gives a level, repeatable surface for launch monitor shots.
