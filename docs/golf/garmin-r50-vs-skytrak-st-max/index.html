How to choose between them

  • Price: the R50 was more than twice the ST MAX's price when we checked.
  • Screen: the R50 has its own screen; check what device the ST MAX needs for its software.
  • Subscriptions: compare each maker's current software terms.
  • Left- and right-handed players: both sit beside the ball, so the unit moves to the other side for a left-hander.
