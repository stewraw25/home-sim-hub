Home golf simulator bays: enclosures, screens and projectors

Updated 2026-11-24. Researched from makers' published documents and Amazon UK listings. We have not tested these products.

A home simulator bay puts a picture of the shot in front of you. It needs a launch monitor plus a few other parts.

The parts of a home bay

  1. Launch monitor to measure the shot. See launch monitors.
  2. Impact screen or net to stop the ball. A screen also takes the projected picture.
  3. Enclosure, a frame with side netting that holds the screen and catches mishits.
  4. Hitting mat to protect the floor. See nets and mats.
  5. Projector, usually a short-throw model so it can sit close to the screen.
  6. Software, from the launch monitor maker or a third party, sometimes on subscription.

Garmin's launch monitor manuals ask for adequate free space behind the net or screen, a net or screen high and wide enough for high or wayward shots, and padding on enclosure frames to reduce ricochet.
