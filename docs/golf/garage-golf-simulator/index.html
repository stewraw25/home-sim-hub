Setting up a golf simulator in a UK garage

Updated 2026-11-24. Researched from makers' published documents and Amazon UK listings. We have not tested these products.

A garage can have more depth than a spare room, which helps with launch monitors that sit behind the golfer. Check the ceiling height carefully, including any garage door track or opener.

1. Measure the space

Measure the lowest point overhead, including any garage door track, opener or beam, then the width and depth. Use the room size guide to compare your figures with each maker's.

2. Pick a launch monitor that suits the depth

Radar units sit behind the golfer and need more depth. Garmin's R10 manual puts it 1.8 to 2.4 m behind the tee, with the ball at least 2.4 m from the net. Camera units such as the Garmin R50 or SkyTrak ST MAX sit beside the ball, which saves depth.

6. Safety checks

  • Garmin's manuals ask for adequate ceiling height, nothing obstructing the swing, free space behind the net or screen, and padding on enclosure frames.
  • Keep cars, windows and anything fragile away from the hitting area.
