About Home Sim Hub

Home Sim Hub is a UK website about setting up home simulators, starting with golf and sim racing.

How we research

  • We use makers' manuals and set-up pages, and Amazon UK listings. Every figure on the site names its source.
  • We have not tested, reviewed hands-on or owned the products we list.
  • We don't give star ratings or scores.
  • Prices are shown as checked on a stated date, because they change often.
